Raku is a great programming language.

In Raku, you can write programs in different styles. They include procedural, functional, object-oriented and reactive programming. Raku has many kinds of types. You can add types step by step. It also has amazing new regexes and grammars. With Raku, many tasks need only very short programs.

This course shows the most important parts of Raku. It also shows many tricks. They make your work faster and easier.
