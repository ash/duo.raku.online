## Problem

Write a program. It finds the sum of the integers from 1 to 100. Both 1 and 100 are part of the sum. Then the program prints the sum.

## Example

Here is the expected result. In this example, the program adds up the numbers from 1 to 10:

```console
$ raku sum1-10.raku
The sum of the numbers from 1 to 10 is 55.
```
