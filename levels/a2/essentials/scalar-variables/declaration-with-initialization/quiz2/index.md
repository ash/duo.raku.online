Here are some short programs, each on one line. In some of them, one statement does two things. It declares a variable and gives it a value. Mark these programs.

{:.quiz}
0 | my $a; $a = 42; | Here, the variable is first created and then a value is assigned to it.
1 | my $a = 42;
1 | my $a = 42; my $b = $a; | You can use the variable to initialize another variable.
