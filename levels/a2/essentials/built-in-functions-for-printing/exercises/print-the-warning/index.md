## Problem

Write a program. It asks for your age. If the number is negative, the program prints one or two warnings. The warning must go to `STDERR`.

## Example

Here are two examples:

```console
$ raku print-the-warning.raku
What is your age? 20
Your age is 20

$ raku print-the-warning.raku
What is your age? -1
You entered a negative number!
```
