## Problem

How many days are there from the 27th of June 2026 to Christmas? Christmas is on the 25th of December 2026. Subtract one date from the other to find the answer. Then write this number of days as weeks and days. First come the full weeks. Then come the days that are left. Print the result in the form below.

## Example

The program prints:

```
25 weeks and 6 days
```
