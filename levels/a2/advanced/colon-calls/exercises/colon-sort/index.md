## Problem

Take the words `pear`, `fig`, `apple`, `kiwi`. Sort them by their **length**. The shortest word comes first, and the longest comes last. Use the colon form of the call. Give the sort key as a Whatever expression, not as a block. The sort key says what to compare. Print the result.

## Example

The program prints:

```
(fig pear kiwi apple)
```
