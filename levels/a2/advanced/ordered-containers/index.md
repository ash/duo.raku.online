Ordered containers keep several elements. They keep the elements in order. So you can get each element by its position. Sometimes they keep only one element, or even no elements at all. The most common ordered containers are _arrays_ and _lists_.

The `@` sigil shows that a variable is for many elements. You can use an index with such a variable. You write the index in square brackets. Then you get one element. You already saw this in the Essentials part.

In this section, we look more closely at arrays and lists. How are they different _as containers_? Which of them can you change? What happens when you put one container inside another? And what is _binding_? Binding gives a name to a container. It copies nothing.
