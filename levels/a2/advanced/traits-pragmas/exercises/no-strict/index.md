## Problem

In Raku, the pragma `strict` is on by default. So you must declare every variable with `my` before you use it. Turn the pragma off with `no strict`. Then assign `10` to `$a` and `20` to `$b`. Do it *without* `my`. Print the sum of the two variables.

## Example

The program prints:

```
30
```
