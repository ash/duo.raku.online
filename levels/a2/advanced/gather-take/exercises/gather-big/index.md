## Problem

Loop over a big range of numbers, from `1` to `100`. Take the numbers one by one. But stop when the next number makes their total bigger than `10`. Print the list of the numbers that you took.

`grep` cannot do this. Here, you must stop the loop in the middle. With `gather` and `take`, you can do it. Use `last` to leave the loop. Do it at the moment you reach the limit. It does not matter how many numbers are still left.

## Example

The program prints:

```
[1 2 3 4]
```
