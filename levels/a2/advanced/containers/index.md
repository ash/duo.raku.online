In Raku, containers keep data. But a container is more than a simple variable. Its role is bigger and deeper. This section shows the different types of containers in Raku.

It is important to understand containers. Without this, some programs can surprise you. Sometimes you expect an array. But the program seems to use a scalar. Or it is the other way around.

Different containers can keep different kinds of data. The data can have a different size. The number of items can be different too. So each type of container has its own methods. They work with the data in the container.
