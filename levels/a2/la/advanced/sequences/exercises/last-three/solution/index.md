Hoc modo munus solvere potes.

## Codex

```raku
say (5, 10 ... 50).tail(3);
```

🦋 Inveni codicem fontem in archivo [last-three.raku](https://github.com/ash/raku-course/blob/master/exercises/advanced/sequences/last-three.raku).

## Exitus

```
(40 45 50)
```

## Commentarii

1. Semina sunt valores initiales: `5, 10`. Gradum quinque dant. Series usque ad finem suum it, `50`.

1. `tail(3)` tres ultimos valores seriei reddit: `40, 45, 50`. Series finita est, itaque finem habet. `head` eodem modo operatur. Primos paucos valores reddit.
