Hoc modo munus solvere potes.

## Codex

```raku
say 'raku'.flip.uc;
```

🦋 Inveni codicem fontem in archivo [reverse-and-shout.raku](https://github.com/ash/raku-course/blob/master/exercises/advanced/strings/reverse-and-shout.raku).

## Exitus

```
UKAR
```

## Commentarii

1. Methodi a sinistra ad dextram operantur. Primum `flip` convertit `'raku'` in `'ukar'`. Deinde `uc` eam in `'UKAR'` convertit.

1. Quaeque methodus novam chordam reddit. Itaque eas in catena in una expressione ponere potes.
