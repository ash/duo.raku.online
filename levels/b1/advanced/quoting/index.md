In the Essentials part, you used [single and double quotes to write strings](/essentials/strings). They are the two most common members of a whole family of _quoting constructs_. With them, you can build strings with exactly the amount of processing you want. This goes from “take every character literally” to “interpolate variables, escapes, and even embedded code”.

This section covers the three base forms `q`, `qq` and `Q`. It also covers the adverbs that adjust them, and _heredocs_ for text with several lines.
