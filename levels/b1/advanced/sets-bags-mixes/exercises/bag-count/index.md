## Problem

A bag is a natural choice for counting words. Take the sentence `the cat sat on the mat`, split it into words, and build a bag from them. Then print two lines. The first is the number of times the word `the` appears. The second is the total number of words.

## Example

The program prints:

```
2
6
```
