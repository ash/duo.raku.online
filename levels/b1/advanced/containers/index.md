title: Understanding Raku containers
---

{% include menu.html %}

In Raku, containers keep data, but they are more than just variables. Their role and meaning go deeper and wider. This section is an overview of the container types that Raku gives you.

It is important to understand the idea of containers. Without it, some programs may surprise you. Sometimes a program seems to use a scalar where you expected an array. Sometimes it is the other way around.

Different containers can keep different kinds of data. The data can also differ in size, or even in the number of items. So each type of container has its own methods for working with its data.
