## Problem

A single `[*]` reduction gives one factorial. Your task is to print _every_ factorial from _1!_ to _6!_ in order. Print them on one line, with just one reduction. Use the triangular form of the reduction meta-operator. It keeps all the products in between, not only the final one.

## Example

The program prints the list of factorials of 1 to 6:

```
(1 2 6 24 120 720)
```
