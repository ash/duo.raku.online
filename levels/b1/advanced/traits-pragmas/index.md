title: Traits and pragmas
---

{% include menu.html %}

Two features let you change how code is **compiled**, before it even runs. A _trait_ is a modifier for a declaration, such as a variable or a routine. You write it with `is`. A _pragma_ is a special instruction that looks like a module. You turn it on with `use`, or off with `no`. It changes a rule for the rest of the lexical scope.

You have already used a trait such as `is default` without paying much attention to it. This section looks at traits as a feature of their own. It shows how to write your own traits and introduces pragmas.
