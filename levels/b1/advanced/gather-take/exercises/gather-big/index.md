## Problem

Loop over a large range of numbers, `1` to `100`, and keep taking them. But stop as soon as their total would become bigger than `10`. Print the list of numbers that you collected.

`grep` cannot do this, because you need to stop the loop before its end. With `gather` and `take` you can do it. Use `last` to leave the loop as soon as you reach the limit. It does not matter how many numbers are left.

## Example

The program prints:

```
[1 2 3 4]
```
