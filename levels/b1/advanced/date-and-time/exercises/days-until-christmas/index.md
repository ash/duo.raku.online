## Problem

Find how many days there are from the 27th of June 2026 to Christmas. Christmas is on the 25th of December 2026. Use date subtraction for this. Then write the number as whole weeks plus the days that are left over. Print it in the form shown below.

## Example

The program prints:

```
25 weeks and 6 days
```
