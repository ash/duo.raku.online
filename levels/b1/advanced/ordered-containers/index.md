Ordered containers keep several elements in order. So you can reach each element by its position. In special cases, they may also keep only one element, or none at all. The most common ordered containers are _arrays_ and _lists_.

The `@` sigil tells you that a variable is for many elements. Such a variable supports subscripting, which means you can read an element by its index. You already saw this in the Essentials part.

In this section, we look more closely at how arrays and lists differ _as containers_. Which of them can you change? What happens when you put one container inside another? And how does _binding_ give a name to a container without copying anything?
