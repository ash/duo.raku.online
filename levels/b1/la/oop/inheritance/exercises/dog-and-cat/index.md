## Problema

Define classem basim `Employee` cum attributo `name` et duabus methodis. Methodus `role` reddit `'staff'`. Methodus `badge` chordam `<name> - <role>` reddit, propria methodo `role` utens.

Deinde define duas classes filias, `Manager` et `Intern`, quae ab `Employee` heredant. Methodum `role` superscribunt: ea `'manager'` in `Manager` reddit et `'intern'` in `Intern`.

Imprime tesseras administratricis nomine `Anna` et tironis nomine `Bob`.

## Exemplum

Programma imprimit:

```
Anna - manager
Bob - intern
```
