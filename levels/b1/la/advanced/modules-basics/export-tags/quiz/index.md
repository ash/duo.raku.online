Modulus `sub hello is export` et `sub goodbye is export(:partings)` declarat. Quae subprogrammata programma directe vocare potest post `use Greeting :partings;`?

{:.quiz}
1 | Solum `goodbye`
0 | Solum `hello`
0 | Et `hello` et `goodbye`
0 | Neutrum eorum

{% include quiz.html %}

<div class="extended-explanation">

Si tesseram nominas, `use` illum gregem *loco* praedefiniti importat. Itaque `:partings` `goodbye` adducit sed `hello` relinquit. Ut utrumque accipias, tesseram inbuiltam `:ALL` peteres per `use Greeting :ALL;`.

</div>
