Hic est una solutio possibilis.

## Codex

```raku
say 'raku'.flip.uc;
```

🦋 Inveni codicem fontem in archivo [reverse-and-shout.raku](https://github.com/ash/raku-course/blob/master/exercises/advanced/strings/reverse-and-shout.raku).

## Exitus

```
UKAR
```

## Commentarii

1. Methodi a sinistra ad dextram currunt. Primum `flip` invertit `'raku'` in `'ukar'`, et deinde `uc` eam in `'UKAR'` convertit.

1. Quaeque methodus novam chordam reddit, itaque eas in una expressione in catenam iungere potes.
