## Problem

Write a program that asks for your age. If the value is negative, it prints one or two warnings. Make sure that the warning goes to `STDERR`.

## Example

Here are two possible cases:

```console
$ raku print-the-warning.raku
What is your age? 20
Your age is 20

$ raku print-the-warning.raku
What is your age? -1
You entered a negative number!
```
