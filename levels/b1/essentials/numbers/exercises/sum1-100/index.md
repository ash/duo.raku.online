## Problem

Write a program that calculates the sum of the integers from 1 to 100. Include both 1 and 100. The program then prints the result.

## Example

Here is the expected result for the sum of the numbers from 1 to 10:

```console
$ raku sum1-10.raku
The sum of the numbers from 1 to 10 is 55.
```
